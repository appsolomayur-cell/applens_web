We Don't Ask for Internet Permission. Period.

Most "privacy apps" paradoxically request full internet access to upload your device statistics and app inventories to remote servers.

AppLens is strictly hardcoded with zero internet permissions. It is technically impossible for the app to send your data, contacts, or APK signatures to the cloud. Everything executes in isolated memory directly on your CPU.

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about security, system permissions, and offline functionality.

To guarantee absolute privacy. Android security enforces permissions at the kernel level. Without the android.permission.INTERNET declaration, it is mathematically impossible for AppLens to transmit any data off your device. All app scanning and decompilation runs purely in local CPU memory.
Starting in Android 11, Google introduced package visibility restrictions. An app manager cannot see installed applications without the QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES permission. AppLens uses this exclusively on your phone to list installed apps and audit their permissions. It never leaves your device.
