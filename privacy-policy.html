About this policy

This policy explains how the AppLens Android app (package com.applens.applens) handles information. AppLens is an app inspector: it shows technical details about the apps installed on your device and checks them for patterns commonly used by fraud apps.

What AppLens reads

  • The list of apps installed on your device, and their details: name, package name, version, size, install and update dates, permissions, components, signing certificate and manifest.
  • APK files you choose to open with AppLens, or that appear in folders you ask it to watch.
  • Only if you grant Usage access: when each app was last used, its foreground time and its storage size.
  • Whether other apps have special access such as Accessibility, device admin, notification access or permission to install unknown apps.

AppLens needs to see all installed apps because inspecting and checking them is its core purpose. It does not read your messages, contacts, photos, location, call logs or accounts.

Where it is processed

  • All analysis and risk checks run entirely on your device.
  • AppLens does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send data to us or to anyone else.
  • There are no accounts, advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services in the app.

What is stored on your device

  • Your settings: whether protection is on, the folders you chose to watch, apps you marked as trusted, and the time of the last check.
  • A short list of APK files found in watched folders, with their check result.
  • Temporary copies of APK files and exported reports while they are being checked or shared. These are kept in the app cache.

All of this is private to AppLens and is deleted when you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. We have no copy of it.

When data leaves your device

  • Only when you choose to save or share a report (PDF, JSON, CSV or copied text). You pick the destination, and the app you share it with handles the file under its own privacy policy.
  • We never sell, rent or share your data, because we never receive it.

Permissions and why they are used

See all installed apps QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES
To list, inspect and risk-check the apps on your device. This is the core function of AppLens.
Usage access, optional PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS
To show last-used time, screen time and storage for an app. You grant it in Android Settings and can turn it off at any time.
Notifications POST_NOTIFICATIONS
To warn you when a risky app is installed or a risky APK file appears.
Run at startup RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED
To keep background checks scheduled after your phone restarts.
Request uninstall REQUEST_DELETE_PACKAGES
To open the Android uninstall screen when you tap Uninstall. Android always asks you to confirm.
Folders you pick
To look for new APK files in folders you select using the Android folder picker. AppLens cannot see any other files.

Your choices

  • Turn off protection on the Protection tab to stop background checks and warnings.
  • Remove watched folders in the app, or revoke Usage access or notifications in Android Settings.
  • Clear the app's storage, or uninstall it, to delete everything it saved.

Security

Because AppLens keeps data in its private app storage and has no network access, your information is protected by Android's app sandbox. Files are only shared outside the app when you explicitly save or share them.

Important notes

  • AppLens is not a malware scanner. A flagged app is not proven malicious, and an app with no flags is not proven safe. Keep Google Play Protect turned on.
  • Helpline numbers and websites mentioned in the app are public government services. AppLens is not affiliated with any government, bank or app developer.
  • AppLens is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly process children's data.
